💡 Korean Dining Etiquette

🚫💵
No Tipping Required!
Service is included in the price, so you don't need to leave a tip in Korea.
🔔
Press the Bell
Most tables have a call bell. If not, say 'Jeogiyo~' (Excuse me) to call staff.
🥢
Cutlery in the Drawer
Check the sliding drawer on the side or under your table for spoons, chopsticks, and napkins.
💧
Water is Usually Self-Serve
If water isn't brought to your table, look for a water purifier (정수기) to get it yourself.
🥗
Free Side Dishes (Banchan)
Side dishes are free and can usually be refilled as many times as you want!
👟
Take Off Your Shoes?
Some traditional restaurants require you to take off your shoes. Watch what others do!
🍲
Sharing is Caring
Many dishes like stews or BBQ are shared from the center. Use the provided serving spoons!
✂️
Scissors are for Food
Don't be surprised! Scissors are commonly used to cut meat, noodles, and kimchi right at the table.
🍚
Leave Your Bowl on the Table
Unlike in some other Asian countries, Koreans usually keep their rice bowls on the table while eating.
👴
Respect the Elders
It is polite to wait for the oldest person at the table to start eating before you do.
